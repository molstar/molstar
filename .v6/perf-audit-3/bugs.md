# Performance audit v3: bugs

Every open bug found or re-verified by the v3 slice audits ([README.md](README.md)), after the verify pass
([\_verify-A](_verify-A.md), [\_verify-B](_verify-B.md), [\_verify-C](_verify-C.md)). Ranked by severity, then by reach.
Ids are `slice/id`. The probe and the fix for each bug are in its slice report's Bugs section.

**42 distinct entries: 0 high, 11 medium, 31 low.**

- **New: 12.** 2 medium (02/B6, 02/B7) and 10 low.
- **Carried over: 30.** These are all 28 entries that v2's final bugs.md still listed as open, plus 06/B7 (partly fixed in v2) and
  13/B2 (the io side of v2's L1).
- **Verify pass:**
  - 01/B17 and 05/B15 were lowered from medium to low.
  - 13/B4 was refuted: `.gro` columns are byte widths, so counting bytes is right.
  - 13/B1 is a duplicate of 01/B10 and is merged into it.
- **Fixes since v2:** the small-fix and leftovers PRs (#2032–#2034, #2044–#2048) closed about 70 bugs from the v2 slice
  reports. None of them was on v2's final open list. **Every medium bug v2 listed is still open.**
- **Bugs introduced by fixes since v2:**
  - 02/B6, from #2034.
  - 02/B7, 05/B15 and 13/B5, from the new code of #2037 and #2040.

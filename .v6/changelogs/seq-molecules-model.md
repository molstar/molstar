## Seq* (next): complexes' units, molecules and atoms as coordinate systems

- A complex's units, molecules and their atoms are coordinate systems: a unit follows, hovers, selects and exports as its
  sequence's range (from 0), copies are derived, links lie on per-row units, atoms collapse onto residues at a path's
  ends; `ResolveConfig.maxUnits` bounds a complex.
- Query forms compose monadically, as MolQL's; new forms `molecule` and `chain :operator :auth` (`elements` and `groups`
  are designed next).

## Seq*: genome navigators

- A navigator over a genome's chromosomes: the window across blocks, the zoom strip of the chromosome in view, the
  readout and slider naming the chromosome; a click jumps to a chromosome (whole from whole, the same width zoomed in);
  the square over blocks × blocks; `navigator.switch: include`; short block labels ("11", "X") thinned, never cut.
- The viewer: `navigate.acrossBlocks`, next and previous chromosome (Alt+PageDown, Alt+PageUp), reverse and show only
  or all from a block's menu, the cursor and drag-select across chromosomes, history by position, the locus box
  falling back to gene names (`findNames`).

## Seq* (next): bridges between sections

- A bridge joins two neighbouring sections with ribbons: an alignment's blocks or a pairs source drawn between two genomes (twisted by strand and block direction, stubs for an end not shown, blocks under a pixel merged, each pair drawn once), with a closed-form hit, export groups and the pending hatch.
- In the viewer a drag or wheel over the band moves both genomes, `lock: px` carries pans and zooms, `bridge.align` (Shift+A) brings the other genome over, `bridge.selectAcross` selects across, a lock toggle, the keyboard cursor across a band and a ribbon's card; Features/Across sections stories (peach, grape, plum). The bridge code loads only when a view has a bridge.

## Seq* (next): one hover contract for every mark

- Every drawn mark answers a hover with a hit, a highlight here and in linked views, and a card, including density bins and bands, matrix cells, carets, a text's group gaps and bridge ribbons; a layer type without a hit is refused (`seq.render.layer.hover`). Legends work both ways (hovering an entry lights its items; an entry with no action shows the default cursor). A VCF allele's card names `pos:ref>alt`, its category, QUAL, FILTER and its INFO fields.

- A row column's cut value reads whole only while the pointer is on its cell (the keyboard cursor on the row's header
  reads them all), never over the row's data from a hover elsewhere on the row.
- The tree's branches draw one width on device pixels; a folded clade is a light, outlined wedge to its deepest leaf.
- Zoomed out, the tree draws down to the clades that resolve, so 10,000 rows no longer draw a solid slab.
- The hidden row columns' chip names them ("Species, Identity hidden"; "2 hidden" short of room), sits in the title band
  on their side and never over the data, and its hover card lists each hidden column and why.

  again.

## Particles

- `Particle.withPoses(list, coordinates, rotations?)`: a copy of a particle list with other poses, recorded as one line
  of the same particles (`ParticleList.posesFrom`, `Particle.isSamePoses`); a trajectory's frame or a drag made so
  updates the particle targets' transforms alone (colors, sizes, the instances' order and the GPU culling's layout
  kept): PetWorld culled on the GPU, a frame 52-57 → 24 ms

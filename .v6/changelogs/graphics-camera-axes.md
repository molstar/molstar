## Graphics: the corner axes

- The camera helper's axes (the corner axes) are slim, shaded, translucent arrows, and the planes small translucent
  squares between them; axes and squares start at the same offset, leaving the origin an open corner (no origin
  sphere). Each is its own render object with no part inside another: one mesh of overlapping rods, caps and planes
  showed its own insides through its translucency.

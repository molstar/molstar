## Seq*: the positions rail's place, and fixes

- The positions rail stays beside a declared navigator (`navigator.positionsRail`: `always`, `unlessNavigator`,
  `none`) and can sit below the ruler, under the tracks or by a navigator (`navigator.railPlace`). A ring's hover draws
  its position box on the ring. An assembly sequence override naming no sequence is reported
  (`seq.resolve.assembly.override`).

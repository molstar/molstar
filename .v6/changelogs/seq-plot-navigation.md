## Seq* (next): a plot's navigation

- A grid's plot keeps a fixed box (width ÷ aspect), whatever its windows.
- Double-click on a cell zooms a step about it on both axes; Alt+Left undoes each step.
- The wheel over a plot pans or zooms it and never scrolls the page; a pan stops at an axis's end.
- A grid over a complex shows chain boundaries as the blocks' gaps, with no line, its cells clipped exactly at each
  block's edge.
- A double-click in the gap between a plot's blocks zooms about that point; the wheel is the plot's over its data box only; a section of blocks and the plot under it align to the pixel at any screen density.

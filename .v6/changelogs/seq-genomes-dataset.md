## Seq\* (next): the Genomes dataset

- The examples gain the Genomes dataset, generated byte-identically: two toy genomes, their alignment and a liftover, genes in GFF3 and GTF, a bedGraph signal, 40 samples' genotypes, methylation and Hi-C, with ten views and their stories.
- A region grid's last tile and a block cut to a range draw their cells at their true positions; a values layer's log scale sets its heights; a log scale over zeros draws; a click on a matrix cell selects its item.

## Prism

- The selection bar is the selection as a menu button and Clear: hover (or a click) opens Focus, Show in structure tree,
  Make a component, the extensions' rows (`ui.selectionActions`) and More in the Selection panel, each with a line on
  what it does.
- Show in structure tree filters the Structure panel's tree by the selection (a chip in its search: only the rows that
  hold it, Contents' entities and chains too; typed text narrows it; its ×, Backspace or clearing the selection end it).

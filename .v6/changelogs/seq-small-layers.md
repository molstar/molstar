## Seq* (next): the small layers

- `ruler`: labels by a numbering at steps of 1, 2 or 5 × 10ⁿ, insertion labels (100A) as minor ticks, counting down on
  a reversed axis; a label that does not fit is skipped, never shrunk.
- `secondary`: helices and strands as blocks, the strand's arrowhead on the piece holding its C-terminal end in every
  direction; `variants`: a stem and a head a site, stacked, a count strip when zoomed out.
- `values` as a `line` or an `area`; `ranges` drawn with a `line` style (solid, dashed, dotted, wavy, double, box,
  highlight) and `arrow` heads; an item a collapsed stretch hides keeps a tick at its break in every layer.

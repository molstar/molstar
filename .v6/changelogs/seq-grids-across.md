## Seq* (next): grids over two sections and categorical axes

- A grid can follow two sections of blocks (`follow_y` naming a second section): a dot plot of two genomes, contacts between two chromosomes' sections. A grid axis can be categorical (`of`/`of_y` an alignment's rows or a matrix's states, ordered by `member_order`/`member_order_y`), with the members' labels down the side and along the top and a card naming both members and the value.

## Seq* (next): the rail's look and hover

- Every rail (ruler, rows, lanes) is a quiet hairline with its window in a mid grey and round knobs that grow about their centres when hovered or dragged (`navigator.rail.tint: 'neutral' | 'accent'`); its ends sit inside the well's rounded corners.
- Hovering a rail shows no card and does not hover its track; a track's header actions show on header hover only (or with the keyboard cursor on it, or its menu open).

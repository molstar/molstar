## Seq* (next): pairs on split copies

- A pair's ends on split copies resolve by unit: a pair on a molecule reaches its units, a pair within one system is
  drawn on every copy, and a caret is hit as its pair. A pair whose data names no units is not drawn on a guessed copy
  (`seq.render.pairs.copy`).
- The hemoglobin example is a complex of 4HHB's units, its heme contacts derived per pair of units.

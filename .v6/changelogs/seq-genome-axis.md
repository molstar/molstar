## Seq*: the genome axis and layouts

- Genomes as blocks on rows and rings: a direction and a scale per block (its own ruler, the direction mark, `×N`),
  two assemblies as groups, gaps by a share, contigs in runs, a hue per block (the `block` and `system` fields); the
  ruler's hover names the block and its position; the places index (`placesOf`) for layers across blocks; SVG export
  with `data-system`, `data-direction`, `data-scale`, `data-assembly`.

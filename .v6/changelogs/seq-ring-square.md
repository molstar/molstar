## Seq\* (next): the genome ring's square

- The square beside a genome ring is a window: drag it along the diagonal, resize it by an edge or corner, Cmd/Ctrl+click cells or labels to show any set of chromosomes, one click on a cell to show that pair; its cells are coloured by link count.
- Stems have a menu and `.` / `,` walk a stem's partners; on one chromosome or a pair, stems are lighter and small partners fold into one stub that unfolds on hover.
- Extensions read their settings with `settingOf`; a source that fails to open reads as failed, not pending.

## Seq* (next): themes and legends

- The `seq.theme` sublanguage on a layer's channels (`color`, `ink`, `size`, `opacity`, `line`, `arrow`, `shape`):
  uniform, field, rules, scheme and letters forms over categorical, direct, binned and continuous (Oklab) palettes;
  shared `scale` nodes; a layer's default theme fixed by the language.
- Built-in residue, nucleotide, numeric and categorical schemes with color-blind-safe substitutes (`theme.cvdSafe`, a
  reader setting); category tokens redesigned for color-vision deficiencies.
- Legends: a Legend part a host places (`mountLegend`), the export's legend band, legend commands; `layer.filter` with an
  "n filtered" chip. The entry `@molstar/seqx-core/theme`.

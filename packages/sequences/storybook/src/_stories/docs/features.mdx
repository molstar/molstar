# Features

## Drawing

- **Rows and lazy alignments.** An [alignment](?path=/docs/seq-docs-concepts--docs#alignment-rows-and-columns)'s rows in tree order, letters over cells, a tree with
  collapsible clades, data columns; loaded region by region as the view moves when lazy. A tree is given (Newick)
  or computed at load from the alignment by the `nj` or `upgma` node provider. An alignment can be
  computed too (`realign`: sequences, units, rows or another alignment's rows through biomsa, each new row bound to its
  input), and `codon_rows` pairs a protein alignment's rows with a cDNA alignment's by `codon` mappings.
  [A tree beside the rows](?path=/story/seq-views-and-layouts-alignments--family-tree),
  [Guide trees and realignment](?path=/story/seq-views-and-layouts-alignments--computed-trees),
  [Large alignment, 10,000 × 300](?path=/story/seq-performance-large-documents--large-alignment),
  [1,000 × 2,000](?path=/story/seq-performance-large-documents--wide-alignment), [case 9](?path=/story/seq-cases-viewer--case-9).
- **Keyed sets: order and filter.** A `rows` layer's `order` takes `"tree"`, `"data"` or a key table's field
  (`{data, field, direction}`, several in a list, lexicographic, ties in data order, missing values last) and its
  `filter` keeps rows by a field's `values` or `min`/`max` (all must pass; `row_subset` first). A key table is a `rows`
  annotation keyed by `row`, or by `state` or `node`; the kernel computes two: `row_stats` (identity to a reference row,
  coverage, length, and the count and best score of an annotation's items on each row) and `redundancy` (greedy
  clustering at an identity threshold: each row's `cluster` and whether it is a `representative`). A track template's
  `for_each` takes the same `order` and `filter` over a table keyed by node, and a `matrix` over one keyed by state.
  The tree is drawn only while the order is the tree's. [Order and filter rows](?path=/story/seq-views-and-layouts-alignments--on-alignment).
- **The vertical overview.** The rows' vertical navigation: a scrollbar, a map of the rows, both,
  or none, with optional zoom handles.
  [Vertical navigation](?path=/story/seq-views-and-layouts-alignments--family-vertical).
- **Row controls.** A host's control per row through the `RowControl` slot.
  [Row controls](?path=/story/seq-views-and-layouts-alignments--row-controls).
- **Coverage in levels.** A `ranges` layer with `display: "union"` draws its items in one lane, their union; a track
  with `summary: true` shows under its group's header only while the group is collapsed. With `split`, a coverage
  nests: all, then each structure, then each chain, every level collapsed to the union of what it holds.
  [p53: sequence and structure](?path=/story/seq-cases-mol--p-53).
- **Per-row marks.** A split track whose rows each sit on a unit of their own (a structure's chains, drawn on a
  reference's axis) marks per row: a highlight, selection or hover on one unit lights its row alone, one on the shared
  sequence every row, and a position hovered on a row is that unit's residue.
  [Per-row marks](?path=/story/seq-interaction-hover--row-marks).
- **Hits by drawn geometry.** A dot larger than its column, a boundary's glyph (an end boundary's from the gap past
  its block too, where nothing else hovers), a pair end or a wide arc hovers where it is drawn (the nearest where
  shapes overlap), a band thinner than its lane only on the band; the tooltip lists
  the fields the layer's color and size themes read ("score 100").
  [Size themes](?path=/story/seq-themes-size-themes--by-scale).
- **Density.** When items are thinner than a pixel, ranges, points and variants draw their count per
  pixel column, stacked by color or the majority color; alignments draw reduced tiles (each pixel
  the most common residue class); values a min–max band and the mean.
  [Genome-scale, 3M nucleotides](?path=/story/seq-performance-large-documents--genome-scale),
  [35,000 residues](?path=/story/seq-performance-large-documents--long-sequence).
- **Values colored by their values.** Bars, a line or an area in the layer's color theme at each value (a column's
  mean zoomed out); a line changes color halfway between two positions, so a pLDDT line reads in its bands; with
  no theme, one color. [Case 8](?path=/story/seq-cases-viewer--case-8).
- **Line styles.** A ranges layer's items solid, dashed, dotted, wavy, double, boxed or highlighted (`line_style`: one
  style for the layer, or a column of the rows through `field_line_style`), in tracks, in the text (a highlight or a
  box also behind the line's letters) and in an SVG export.
- **Logo.** Stacked letters by bits or probability. [Case 3](?path=/story/seq-cases-viewer--case-3).
- **Pairs.** Arcs within and between blocks, ends with glyphs, all or on hover.
  [Case 4](?path=/story/seq-cases-viewer--case-4), [case 7](?path=/story/seq-cases-viewer--case-7).
- **Matrix.** Positions × states as tiled images, a band per state. Thousands of states (a genotype matrix's samples)
  thinner than a device pixel draw a row per device pixel, by the layer's `density`: the plurality of its states'
  colors, faded by the share of its states without a color (`majority`), or its strongest value (`maximum`). Its
  height follows the row zoom (Alt and the wheel).
  A matrix's `order` and `filter` take a key table keyed by `state`, as a `rows` layer's do.
  [A genotype matrix](?path=/story/seq-cases-viewer--case-17).
  [Case 7](?path=/story/seq-cases-viewer--case-7).
- **Dot plots.** Positions against positions on one section's axis (`dot_plot`): x across as every track's, and the
  same window down the track, so zooming and panning the tracks moves both. A `grid` (a value per pair of positions,
  AlphaFold DB's predicted aligned error read by `molstar:pae`) as cells in a color theme, the mean of the cells a device
  pixel holds when zoomed out; `pairs` as segments, a dot for one position each. A hover on a cell lights both
  positions (a 3D view both residues) with a crosshair; a drag selects a rectangle as two ranges. Square by default
  (`aspect_ratio`, its height from the data area's width, at most what a box shows).
  [Case 8](?path=/story/seq-cases-viewer--case-8), [calmodulin against itself](?path=/story/seq-views-and-layouts-dot-plots--self-comparison).
- **The fingerprint.** The interactions extension's renderer, a registry entry like the built-ins.
  [Case 6](?path=/story/seq-cases-viewer--case-6).
- **The glyph atlas.** Letters drawn from an atlas of rasterized cells; frame times with it on and
  off, and the pixels compared. [Glyph atlas](?path=/story/seq-performance-large-documents--glyph-atlas).
- **`style` and render props.** A section's [`style`](?path=/docs/seq-docs-concepts--docs#theme-preset-and-style) as a preset name,
  or an inline preset overriding a few of its render props (row height, header width, …).
  [Column heads](?path=/story/seq-views-and-layouts-section-style--column-heads),
  [data floor](?path=/story/seq-views-and-layouts-section-style--data-floor), [an invalid key](?path=/story/seq-views-and-layouts-section-style--invalid-key).
- **[Pieces](?path=/docs/seq-docs-concepts--docs#piece) and direction.** A block shows a `range` of its coordinate system in a `direction`;
  a reversed piece is a layout map, never new data. [Direction of a piece](?path=/story/seq-views-and-layouts-axes-and-strands--direction).
- **[Cyclic systems](?path=/docs/seq-docs-concepts--docs#cyclic-systems).** A `cyclic` sequence; ranges across its
  origin (`start > stop`), a block's `origin`, a focus on the shortest arc, lanes packed by arc. [A plasmid, linear](?path=/story/seq-views-and-layouts-axes-and-strands--linear).
- **Rings.** A section with the `circular` style draws its tracks as rings, the axis clockwise from the top: every
  track as it draws linearly, mapped onto the circle; hits by radius and angle. The ruler's numbers sit outside the
  ring with minor ticks between them; selections and highlights are sectors over every ring, the hover a radial line
  or the hovered item's sector; the keyboard cursor goes round; `renderSvg` exports the ring. A type that draws
  images (a matrix, dense rows) is drawn as bars there, with a `svs.render.fallback` warning.
  [A plasmid, ring](?path=/story/seq-views-and-layouts-axes-and-strands--ring).
- **Circular maps.** On rings, the labels of a `ranges` layer with `labels` sit outside the ring past the ruler's
  numbers, each with a leader that leaves the ring on its item's angle and fans out to it (sorted by angle, a line
  apart per side; those a side cannot hold are hidden, the layer's order then the longer item kept first, and the
  section says so once, `seqv.render.labels`). A `direction_field` (`+`/`-`, `1`/`-1`, `forward`/`reverse`) draws
  an arrowhead at each item's end, on rings and linearly. `pairs` are chords through the hub. Hovering or clicking a
  label hovers or selects its item. The presets `b.preset.plasmidMap` (its cut sites as ticks on the features'
  ring) and `b.preset.circularGenome` write a ring and a linear section following it.
  [A plasmid map](?path=/story/seq-cases-viewer--case-16),
  [A circular genome](?path=/story/seq-views-and-layouts-axes-and-strands--circular-genome).
- **The ring's window.** A ring linked by `follow` to a linear section shows that section's view as a window: a
  sector over the rings with a grip at each end on the rim. It follows the linear view; dragging it pans the linear
  section, dragging a grip zooms it with the other end held, within the section's zoom limits and its content; across
  a cyclic origin it wraps. A ring never pans or zooms itself: its `+` and `-` zoom the linear section about the
  window's center, and it leads its followers only through the window.
  [A plasmid map](?path=/story/seq-cases-viewer--case-16).
- **Letter maps.** A layer's table from the stored letter to the one shown (`letter_map`: a name such as `complement`,
  or single letters mapped); the data keeps its letters. A strand flip is a reversed piece plus the `complement` map
  (`b.preset.reverseComplement`), an mRNA is `{ T: U }`, and `b.preset.exonsOnly` collapses a gene to its exons.
  [Letter maps](?path=/story/seq-views-and-layouts-axes-and-strands--maps).
- **Color schemes.** Sixteen named schemes (Clustal X, Zappo, Taylor, hydrophobicity, percent identity,
  three for nucleotides, Viridis, Cividis, Magma, Blues, Red–blue, Category 10, Okabe–Ito, Tableau 10),
  each fitting letters of its alphabets, values or categories, with a dark variant and a colorblind-safe
  flag; picked in the document (`{"theme": "scheme"}`), in the builder (`b.colors`), at runtime (a
  legend group's title, a track's Colors ▸) or by the `seqv:colorScheme` command; and a document's or a
  host's own. The legend lists only tracks with something to say, identical legends merged.
  [Color schemes](?path=/docs/seq-docs-color-schemes--docs), [gallery](?path=/story/seq-themes-color-schemes--gallery),
  [live picker](?path=/story/seq-themes-color-schemes--picker), [your own palette](?path=/story/seq-themes-color-schemes--custom-palette),
  [many tracks](?path=/story/seq-themes-color-schemes--many-tracks).
- **Hover emphasis.** The element under the pointer, and only it, is outlined inside its own pixels in an ink derived
  from its color: darker on a light skin, lighter on a dark one, 3:1 against the surface (a range's every part, a site,
  a variant, a strand, a score bar, a matrix cell, a fingerprint's stripe, never over letters; the keyboard cursor's item
  too). Off per section with `"style": {"hover_emphasis": false}`; an extension's renderer gets it in one call
  (`outlineItem`, `ringItem`). [Hover emphasis](?path=/story/seq-interaction-hover--emphasis), [off](?path=/story/seq-interaction-hover--off).
- **Header hover.** The `headerHover` behavior of the viewer spec: a feature track's header lights the items it shows
  (a split row's header that row's, a group's header its tracks'), a continuous track's (letters, values) its row band
  alone, and past `maxItems` the row band alone; linked views get the set, sized first (`hover.size()`) so a 3D link
  can summarize a large one as whole chains (`linkViews`' options). The p53 demo composes both: a lower `maxItems` in
  its viewer spec, its link's own limit. [The Mol\* demo](?path=/story/seq-cases-mol--p-53).
- **Row band.** The `hoverRow` behavior: a hover anywhere on a track's data lights its row at half a header hover's
  strength (a matrix its hovered band, the ruler and a dot plot none), a drawing only, so a 3D link never reads it as the header's hover.
  Rows have no borders unless a document sets `"style": {"row_borders": true}` (a host: `rowBorders`, `renderSvg`'s `render` too).

## Interaction

- **Selecting text.** A text view selects as an editor does: a click selects a residue (again, clears it; a double
  click is a click); a drag selects positions line by line to the nearest one under the pointer, outside the canvas
  too, scrolling a box past its edges; a click on an underline selects its item. In every section
  Shift with a click or a drag adds or removes an item or a position (a drag removing when pressed on a selected
  one); with `shiftPositions: "extend"` it extends a range of positions or columns (made in any view) from the anchor
  instead, keeping its far end; Ctrl/⌘ adds or removes; where a double click zooms, a click selects once the double-click time has passed, so a
  double click never selects. The text sits inset from the canvas's edges, in the `wrapped` or the `compact` preset.
  [p53: text and tracks](?path=/story/seq-cases-viewer--case-1) (its Text choice switches the preset).
- **Text for a host.** Render props shape the text view: `wrappedAlign: "center"` and `wrappedMaxWidth` (a centered
  reading column), `wrappedColors` (the letters in the layer's colors), and `unobservedGray` (a structure's unobserved
  residues in the skin's unobserved color, in text and in cells). A focus scrolls the text to its lines
  (`focusViewport`'s `reveal`), and a new text section opens on its focus. A host that opens its own track menu at
  the pointer adds the `contextMenu` behavior (`SeqViewerOptionalBehaviors`): a secondary press asks for it.
- **Follow.** A section follows another's window through a coordinate map, a codon step's third
  included. [Following sections](?path=/story/seq-views-and-layouts-axes-and-strands--follow).
- **Patches, snapshots, undo.** Filter view, hide and remove, each a patch (a small change to the
  document, addressed by refs); snapshots as patches over one document, and their player. [Filter view, hide and remove](?path=/story/seq-interaction-patches-and-snapshots--viewer-patches),
  [the same through SeqViewer](?path=/story/seq-interaction-patches-and-snapshots--seq-viewer-patches).
- **The default UIs.** The same parts and skin in React and as `<seq-viewer>`; a host's markdown
  with command links through `@molstar/seq-core/links`. Every piece of chrome (tabs, a section's switches,
  the legend, export, issues, the player, search) is a component a host places itself, beside the viewer
  or in its own toolbar; `chrome: "full"` and `"clean"` are the viewer's own compositions of them. The
  look is canvas chrome: glass strips and menus over the canvas, an accent wash for the picked item,
  following the canvas's light or dark.
  [React UI](?path=/story/seq-cases-default-uis--case-10),
  [the chrome placed by the host](?path=/story/seq-cases-default-uis--composed),
  [DOM UI, description links](?path=/story/seq-cases-default-uis--description-links),
  [the DOM's](?path=/story/seq-cases-default-uis--dom-composed).
- **Search.** A library of matchers (exact, degenerate, regular expression, Myers' approximate) that an application
  composes; here into a box that lights and steps through hits.
  [App-composed search](?path=/story/seq-query-and-search-search--app-composed-search). The viewer's own `Search` part appears
  when the UI spec gives `search(text, signal)`: its answer goes to `ctx.results`, lit, stepped with Enter and
  Shift+Enter, and saved as a location set; a regular-expression motif such as `[KR]{2}.{10,12}[KR]{3,}` (p53's bipartite
  nuclear localization signal) searches the letters. [Finding things in p53](?path=/story/seq-cases-viewer--case-18).
- **Location sets and bookmarks.** A section's `highlights` show annotations (a SeqQL query node
  among them) persistently, each entry a highlight channel: a band behind the tracks, or the
  overlay's highlight or accent; Nightingale lights them as its `highlight`. Bookmarks are an inline
  ranges set the `bookmark.add` (with a label and a `color`), `bookmark.remove` and `bookmark.step` actions edit and step through; the `Annotations` part (`/react`, `/dom`) is that as a panel: add the selection with a label and a color, a ranges track over the set draws it, in the text view and the tracks view alike ([Publication tracks](?path=/story/seq-text-view--publication-tracks)). [Location sets and bookmarks](?path=/story/seq-query-and-search-search--location-sets),
  [finding things in p53](?path=/story/seq-cases-viewer--case-18).
- **The hover card.** Items with a host's actions (`UI.ItemActions`) get an interactive card after a short rest: the
  hover's label and a button per action; it stays while the pointer crosses into it, and closes on leaving, Escape,
  a press outside, a pan or a zoom. The tooltip keeps its rule (it never takes the pointer). From the keyboard, the
  track menu (Shift+F10) lists the cursor's item's actions first. A host's track menu items can carry a check mark
  (`checked`).
- **Structures (Mol\*).** `@molstar/seq-molstar-poc` (a proof of concept) reads structures headless, maps their chains onto
  UniProt by SIFTS, and links a Mol\* canvas: hover and selection both ways, "Show in 3D" from the hover card and the
  track menu, and the structure colored by any track as drawn (MVS color tables). Its providers give pLDDT and the
  model's own secondary structure (`molstar:secondary_structure`); a hover or selection on a ligand lights the whole
  ligand in 3D (the link's `wholeResidue` reaction).
  [p53: sequence and structure](?path=/story/seq-cases-mol--p-53).
- **The query language.** SeqQL's reader, binder, checker and printer (`(search "[KR]{2}.{10,12}[KR]{3,}" :in p53 :regex true)` finds a motif by regular expression): an S-expression box with the canonical
  print, the JSON and how each short name bound; and its runtime, a gallery of queries run on the cases.
  [Read, bind, check, print](?path=/story/seq-query-and-search-seqql--read-bind-print),
  [runtime gallery](?path=/story/seq-query-and-search-seqql--gallery).

## Data

- **File formats.** Each family of formats and services loading, in the `File formats` folder: the nodes that load
  it, the data as given, the viewer and what loading reported.
  [FASTA and residues](?path=/story/seq-file-formats--sequences),
  [aligned FASTA, A3M, Stockholm, Clustal and Newick](?path=/story/seq-file-formats--alignments),
  [TSV, CSV and JSON tables](?path=/story/seq-file-formats--tables) (`fields`, `ins_code`),
  [mmCIF and BinaryCIF](?path=/story/seq-file-formats--structures) (CIF categories as tables),
  [UniProt](?path=/story/seq-file-formats--uni-prot) and [PDBe and AlphaFold DB](?path=/story/seq-file-formats--pdbe-afdb)
  (recorded, or live from the public APIs),
  [2bit, BigWig, BigBed and tabix VCF](?path=/story/seq-file-formats--genome-files) streamed by byte ranges,
  [the tiles protocol](?path=/story/seq-file-formats--tiles), and
  [`.svsj`, `.svsm` and `.svsx`](?path=/story/seq-file-formats--documents).
- **Streaming.** Data too large to load whole comes by region from a stream provider (the
  [Streaming](?path=/docs/seq-docs-streaming--docs) page explains it, with a live provider): a
  manifest first (what the source holds), then requests for the view and a margin around it,
  coarse before fine, with a byte-bounded cache that keeps what is in view; its check is a bench, not a story: GRCh38's 24 chromosomes
  as a streamed axis (`viewer/_test/canvas/hg38.test.ts`). The `seqstar-tiles/1` client is a stream
  provider: [case 9](?path=/story/seq-cases-viewer--case-9) loads through it. Streamed letters: a chromosome's sequence by region, as cells and letters once zoomed in
  ([Streamed data](?path=/story/seq-streaming-streamed-data--letters)); streamed genes and variants as items
  region by region or as density from counts, with placeholder bands while regions load.
  Progressive loading: skeletons that shimmer, error bands with Retry, a fade as regions arrive, "loading…"
  on hover; streamed items hovered and colored by a field's palette. The genome browser demo: hg38 read live
  from 2bit, BigWig, BigBed and tabix VCF files ([the demo](?path=/story/seq-cases-genome-browser--live)), zoomed out from the
  files' zoom levels and indexes only, the zoom gate's hints and "click to load" (what is in view), genes folded
  into a lane a gene where their lanes would not fit (`collapse`; "Expand rows" shows every transcript). The
  [ideogram navigator](?path=/story/seq-views-and-layouts-navigators--karyotype): each chromosome as its cytobands (named data,
  hg38's committed), the view's window over it.
  [Prefetch](?path=/story/seq-streaming-prefetch--ahead): regions asked ahead of a pan or zoom and for the next snapshots'
  focus, the request log beside the view. [A protein on its genome](?path=/story/seq-streaming-streamed-data--through-a-path):
  a genome's streamed genes, variants and scores on a protein's axis through its codon mapping, and a hovered residue's
  codon in its label (its bases from the streamed letters, both parts of one an intron splits, its positions and strand).
  [A protein on its streamed transcript](?path=/story/seq-streaming-streamed-data--on-its-transcript): a streamed
  item with a `map` becomes a coordinate system of its own (`genes_src/T1`); a protein mapped onto it shows its
  domains along the transcript's exons, and a section of the transcript's residues shows the genome's data.
- **Dynamic nodes.** A streamed source's manifest and patches add data nodes to the set, never to
  the document: checked as document nodes, held by (source, key), and evicted with the
  regions that brought them once nothing shows them; a track naming one appears when it arrives.
  [Dynamic nodes: manifest, patch and eviction](?path=/story/seq-streaming-streamed-data--first-part); how they work in
  [Streaming](?path=/docs/seq-docs-streaming--docs).
- **Nested sources and track templates.** A manifest or patch may add streamed sources of its own,
  asked for only when needed and never past `max_depth`; a track with `for_each` shows one track per
  dynamic node it selects (by kind, source and depth), at its place in key order, and an arrival
  reruns only its template and the view.
  [Dynamic nodes: templates and nested sources](?path=/story/seq-streaming-streamed-data--second-part).
- **At scale.** [Genome-scale, 3M nucleotides](?path=/story/seq-performance-large-documents--genome-scale) (a load under 1.5 s, a frame under
  16 ms).

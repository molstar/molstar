# Genome ring

A genome ring is a Rondo-like circular view of a genome's links: chromosomes as arcs, cytobands and a hue ring outside,
links bundled into **ribbons** between **stems** inside. It is an extension, `@molstar/seqx-ext-rondo`: the headless
`rondo` (the `rondo:ribbons` layer type, the `rondo:hues` scheme, `rondoRing`) and `rondoViewer` (its commands and a
binding). A reader without it draws the same spec with core's pairs.

## What a ring is made of

- **Stems**: a coarser level of the pairs pyramid, `2^j` times the level read, the largest no wider than `rondo.stemPx`
  (16 CSS px) at the ring's inner edge. Exact counts, and the same stems on screen, in the SVG and at any DPR. A stem's
  mouth narrows to a neck: its width is the sum of its ribbons, stubs and fan, one CSS px a link at the largest stem in
  view (`rondo.maxWidthPx`, 24 px).
- **Ribbons**: one a stem pair and a strand, wide by count, the strongest drawn first (at the bottom of the stack). A
  ribbon whose ends run opposite ways is crossed (the twist by the ends' low and high edges). Ribbons under a device
  pixel, or past `rondo.maxRibbons` (1,024), go to their stem's **fan**: its total kept, its partners named in the card.
- **Stubs**: links to a chromosome the ring does not show (a section's `include`) end in a short taper inward, one a
  stem a partner, in the partner's hue, labelled at the tip where there is room.
- **The hole** is the ribbons' (`center: none`, written by `rondoRing`): no title there.
- **Bins, then items**: the layer reads bins until the links in view are at most `rondo.maxRibbons`, then the items (the
  same stems; widths by the items' count). While bins are pending the hole shows core's hatch, never "no links".

## Colour

A hue a chromosome by its `a` end's system, in the assembly's order before any `include`, so a chromosome has one hue on
every ring. The default is the safe `categories` (repeating past eight); `rondo:hues` gives 24 distinct hues, not
colour-blind safe, with `categories` as its substitute. In the dark theme its hues are derived from the light ones.

A layer may write another colour: a fixed one (every ribbon, stem and stub in it) or a field read link by link (at the
item level a ribbon a value; at the bins level, where a bin sums links of any value, the layer's one colour, said when
its links' values are mixed); `opacity` makes a layer faint.

## Kinds of links

A link's geometry says nothing of what it means (a 3D contact, a translocation, a fusion, synteny): the kind is in the
data (`type`), so a view shows it through layers. `rondoRing({ kinds })` writes a `rondo:ribbons` layer a kind over the
same links, each its data filter on `type`, its track, name, colour, width rule and legend entry, the last listed drawn
on top; a card names a ribbon's kind, strands, `q` and genes, and a bin's card its count on each layer. Which kinds a
view layers is the application's: the showcase draws the contacts faint in the hues and five made-up translocations (one
a chr9–chr22 junction in the style of BCR-ABL1) in the accent over them.

## Opening a chromosome or a pair

A cut, not a zoom: a section's `include` names the chromosomes shown (one patch, undone by undo). With `opens`, a
double-click on a ribbon, a stem or a stub runs `rondo:open` (a binding scoped to the ribbons' hits, winning over the
focus there only); the square beside the ring (`navigator.switch: include`) shows a cell's pair; a label's menu has
"Show only".

## Words

The layer type gives the hover card its lines (a ribbon: its two ends and its count; a stem: its links and partners),
the ARIA description of its group (the ribbons, chromosomes and the strongest pair) and what the keyboard cursor adds at
a stem ("stem of 140 links").

## Without the extension

The same spec draws the ribbons as core's `pairs` (bin-pair chords over the same bins), canvas and SVG alike; the issues
say once that the layer needs `rondo` at its version. `viewer.extend(rondoViewer)` draws the ribbons, its undo the
chords again.

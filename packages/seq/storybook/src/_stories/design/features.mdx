# Features

What this version contains and will contain. Each feature links its story here once it runs.

## Built

- **Editing**: every edit is a patch, and patches are the only undoable changes.
  - patches through the viewer's commands (hide, show, move a track, color a layer, add a bookmark), each one step of a
    host's undo stack that also holds the host's own state:
    [Features › Editing](?path=/story/seq-next-features-editing--editing);
  - undo and redo through the host, in order, the host's steps between the viewer's, and a track added as one patch, the
    SVG export following: the same story;
  - previews: a drag's transient change, each move replacing the last, ending in one patch on release or nothing on Esc:
    the same two stories;
  - rebase: a patch that lands mid-gesture leaves the preview over the new spec, and drops it when what it resizes is
    gone: tested, not shown in a story.
- **The small layers**: the ruler, secondary structure, variants, values as a line and an area, ranges as lines and with
  arrowheads, each drawn by the canvas and by `renderSvg` from one spec and hit alike, on a reversed or collapsed axis
  as on a plain one:
  - the ruler: labels at steps of 1, 2 or 5 × 10ⁿ that never overlap, insertion labels (100A) as minor ticks where there
    is room, the numbering a spec param (a built-in or a sequence's declared one: Kabat, IMGT), a reversed axis counting
    down;
  - secondary structure: helices and strands as blocks, the strand's arrowhead on the piece holding its C-terminal end
    whatever the direction, the origin or the codon map, 200 copies drawn as one;
  - variants: a stem and a head a site, the heads of one site stacked, a count strip where sites are under a pixel;
  - values as a line (held over half cells at its ends and gaps, broken by missing values) and an area;
  - ranges' line styles anchored to their content and arrowheads on the ends they point to:
    [Features › Layers](?path=/story/seq-next-features-layers--ruler), one story a layer, in the light and the dark
    theme;
  - each track in its well, a squircle its data is clipped to, with a padding inside it (top, bottom or both), and every
    emphasis a squircle:
    [Features › Layers › Tracks in their wells](?path=/story/seq-next-features-layers--tracks-in-their-wells).
- **Addressing**: addresses as `path/(query :key value)`, resolved and printed back.
  - refs and bookmarks, on dynamic nodes too (they wait until the node arrives, then resolve):
    [Features › Addressing › Bookmarks](?path=/story/seq-next-features-addressing-bookmarks--bookmarks), with a short
    text whose links move the view the same way;
  - links in a text that focus, highlight and select; one residue in two numberings; copy link:
    [Features › Addressing › Links](?path=/story/seq-next-features-addressing-links--links);
  - the selection and the focus as addresses: the status lines of the Links and Selection stories.
- **Selection**: the default gesture grammar.
  - click, shift and toggle clicks, drag-select, several ranges (Cmd/Ctrl+drag adds, Alt/Option+drag removes, Shift
    extends the anchor's range), Esc; the keyboard cursor with its arrows, Space and Enter:
    [Features › Selection](?path=/story/seq-next-features-selection--tracks);
  - projection: a selection on one section shown on another through the maps, lighter:
    [Features › Selection › Protein on its gene](?path=/story/seq-next-features-selection--protein-on-its-gene): a drag
    on the gene's letters, the protein showing the codons, in the canvas and in the SVG;
  - the selection drawn as one band a section (column) or a box a track (tracks): the shape toggle of those stories.
- **Navigation**: pan and zoom by hand (drag, wheel, pinch), jumps that animate (focus, fit, reveal, step zooms; a cut
  under reduced motion), and the view's history of jumps: a double-click on what is focused goes back,
  Shift+double-click and `-` zoom out, `0` fits, Alt+Left, Backspace or the mouse's back button go back, Alt+Right
  forward: [Features › Selection](?path=/story/seq-next-features-selection--tracks).
- **Security bounds**: a spec comes from a URL, a link or an agent, so what it sizes is refused with a coded issue
  before the work starts: its size (64 MB), depth (256), query depth (64), column length and block values (2^24); the
  issues kept are bounded too (the first 10 of a code, at most 100 a node). The viewer holds them on its patches,
  previews, story steps and whole roots (a load rejects with the issues), and its store on tables and nodes: tested, not
  shown in a story.

- **The axis**: a section's direction (`genomic`, `reversed`, following another), another system's letters on a section
  (a protein a codon wide on its gene), collapsed introns of zero width on every layout:
  [Features › Coordinates](?path=/story/seq-next-features-coordinates--protein-and-gene-on-one-axis).
- **Themes and legends**: field palettes, rules, schemes (Clustal X, ESPript, `classes` and its safe substitute), shared
  domains as `scale` nodes, size channels, a reader's color-blind substitution, legends a host places:
  [Features › Themes](?path=/story/seq-next-features-themes--themes).
- **Layouts**: tracks whose heights fit their content, text and alignment figures, blocks (a row, a stack, chips of one
  position), rings with a window a linear detail follows, through one layout interface:
  [Features › Text](?path=/story/seq-next-features-text--text),
  [Examples › Complex › Blocks](?path=/story/seq-next-examples-complex-blocks--row),
  [Examples › Plasmid › Ring](?path=/story/seq-next-examples-plasmid-ring--ring).
- **Navigators**: the ruler rail on every linear section, strips, an ideogram, a vertical overview, a miniature, a zoom
  strip: [Features › Navigators](?path=/story/seq-next-features-navigators--rail).
- **Pairs, grids and dot plots**: arcs above and below, chords on a ring, crossing pairs split, a grid's own section
  with its crosshair, rectangles and navigation, linked pairs lit from a hovered or selected range:
  [Examples › tRNA](?path=/story/seq-next-examples-trna-views--arcs),
  [Examples › Complex › PAE and contacts](?path=/story/seq-next-examples-complex-pae-and-contacts--pae).
- **The logo and the rows layer**: a conservation logo, an alignment's rows beside their tree, clades folded to a
  summary row, row columns (a row's fields, identity, length) ordered by a title's click, pinned rows, 10,000 rows held
  and millions lazy: [Examples › Globins › Rows](?path=/story/seq-next-examples-globins-rows--family),
  [Features › Rows](?path=/story/seq-next-features-rows--columns).
- **Files and formats**: FASTA, aligned FASTA, Stockholm, Newick, and tables as TSV, CSV, JSON, MessagePack and GFF3,
  one source over several sequences: [Features › Files](?path=/story/seq-next-features-files--uniprot-entry); queries
  over them: [Features › Query](?path=/story/seq-next-features-query--query-box).
- **Numbering schemes**: a sequence's declared numbering (Kabat, IMGT) read by queries, addresses, the ruler and the
  hover, insertions labelled, the positions it does not number marked:
  [Examples › Antibody](?path=/story/seq-next-examples-antibody-views--schemes).
- **Interaction**: the keyboard cursor over an alignment's rows and a grid's y; the hover card and the keys panel (a
  port a host can replace); overlapping highlight channels; copying the selection's letters as shown:
  [Features › Hover card and keys](?path=/story/seq-next-features-hover-card-and-keys--hover-card),
  [Features › Highlights](?path=/story/seq-next-features-highlights--overlapping-channels).
- **Stories**: steps as full states or patches, a player on the application's clock, a step's view:
  [Features › Story](?path=/story/seq-next-features-story--story).
- **Export**: SVG and PNG, the same picture as the canvas, with an accessible structure; the `seqx-render` command line.
- **The datasets**: p53 (its tracks, its gene, a text figure, its chromosome by region), Globins, Plasmid, tRNA, Complex
  and Antibody, each with an index of its sources and licences:
  [Examples › p53 › Chromosome](?path=/story/seq-next-examples-p53-chromosome--chromosome).
- **The structure link**: hover, selection and focus both ways with a structure viewer (a fake here, its host's adapter
  over `structureFacts`), chain maps by `priority` and `via`, a structure colored by a track:
  [Features › Structure link](?path=/story/seq-next-features-structure-link--hover-both-ways).
- **Host data**: a host serves its own data through a data adapter (`withAdapter`), no file read, and an edit reopens
  the source in place: [Features › Host data](?path=/story/seq-next-features-host-data--served-by-the-fake).
- **Track headers**: actions beside a track's label (drawn or as DOM parts), the state chips and marks, a host's entry
  and widget: [Features › Track headers](?path=/story/seq-next-features-track-headers--built-ins).
- **A minimal viewer**: the layer types and layouts a spec lists, nothing else in its bundle.
- **Extensions**: an extension's descriptor (its id the namespace of everything it ships), a layer type named
  `<id>:<name>` with its fallback, its plain params and its keys on built-in nodes, a scheme, a command and a binding;
  a reader without it draws the fallback and says which extension the figure needs; registered and taken away at run
  time: [Features › Extensibility](?path=/story/seq-next-features-extensibility--toggled-at-run-time).

## Planned

- **Language**: validation shown on the Antibody view's invalid labels; versions and migrations; the language's docs and
  agent skill.
- **Coordinates and maps**:
  - numbering schemes beyond labels (offset, segments);
  - map kinds beyond offset and codon (numbering, segments, pairwise, alignment, structure), computed maps;
  - structure maps (SIFTS in abstract form) and two maps of one pair chosen by `priority` or `via` (the fourth slice);
  - pairs, ranges and maps on an alignment's columns (the fourth slice, 0.5.0).
- **Data and sources**:
  - molecules: `complex` and `unit` kinds, copies addressed by unit (the fourth slice);
  - A3M and Clustal; the matrix shape (with the Genomes milestone);
  - patches on streamed things and a command to keep a region; patch streams from an agent or a host;
  - a source's assembly; bigWig read by region, as proof that a genome format plugs in.
- **Layers and themes**: track groups and summaries.
- **Interaction and editing**: search and its results; track and group editing beyond hide, reorder and themes; the
  context menu with track header parts (the fourth slice); drags snapped to whole codons.
- **Rendering**: a second consumer of the resolved view (the test consumer); the glyph atlas and band copy.
- **Ports with default adapters**: structure syncing as a library (the fourth slice); archives over the assets port; seq
  data ports (the fourth slice).
- **Configuration and extensions**:
  - one typed configuration (settings in layers and a generated form come later);
  - layout and spacing in presets;
  - node kinds and custom fields;
  - every registry open.
- **Addressing**: rects as addresses; one href driving Mol\* too.
- **Integration and showcases**:
  - an application composing services (UniProt, PDBe, AlphaFold DB) and reading structure files;
  - the Mol\* link on a fake structure viewer first (the fourth slice): a lab's position-indexed predictions mapped onto
    a structure's `label` and `auth` numbering.

## Deferred

Each is deferred until the trigger named for it.

- **A published JSON Schema**: until an outside reader or an agent tool needs one.
- **The Genomes milestone**: layers across sections and synteny, contact maps, a MAF over a genome, values for genomics
  (points, split, log scale), variants by sample, matrices, keyed joins, gene hierarchies, and the genome formats beyond
  bigWig: after version 1's views run.
- **Writers (FASTA, PDF)**: until the first writer extension.
- **Agent tools**: until the language description and addressing are built.
- **A React adapter**: until a React host asks.

# Features

What this version contains and will contain. Each feature links its story here once it runs.

## Built

- **Editing**: every edit is a patch, and patches are the only undoable changes.
  - patches through the viewer's commands (hide, show, move a track, color a layer, add a bookmark), each one step of a
    host's undo stack that also holds the host's own state:
    [Features › Editing](?path=/story/seq-next-features-editing--editing);
  - undo and redo through the host, in order, the host's steps between the viewer's: the same story, and on p53
    [Edit and undo](?path=/story/seq-next-examples-p53-edit-and-undo--edit-and-undo);
  - previews: a drag's transient change, each move replacing the last, ending in one patch on release or nothing on Esc:
    the same two stories;
  - rebase: a patch that lands mid-gesture leaves the preview over the new spec, and drops it when what it resizes is
    gone: tested, not shown in a story.
- **The small layers**: the ruler, secondary structure, variants, values as a line and an area, ranges as lines and with
  arrowheads, each drawn by the canvas and by `renderSvg` from one spec and hit alike, on a reversed or collapsed axis
  as on a plain one:
  - the ruler: labels at steps of 1, 2 or 5 × 10ⁿ that never overlap, insertion labels (100A) as minor ticks where there
    is room, the numbering a spec param, a reversed axis counting down;
  - secondary structure: helices and strands as blocks, the strand's arrowhead on the piece holding its C-terminal end
    whatever the direction, the origin or the codon map, 200 copies drawn as one;
  - variants: a stem and a head a site, the heads of one site stacked, a count strip where sites are under a pixel;
  - values as a line (held over half cells at its ends and gaps, broken by missing values) and an area;
  - ranges' line styles anchored to their content and arrowheads on the ends they point to:
    [Features › Layers](?path=/story/seq-next-features-layers--ruler), one story a layer, in the light and the dark
    theme.
- **Addressing**: addresses as `path/(query :key value)`, resolved and printed back.
  - refs and bookmarks, on dynamic nodes too (they wait until the node arrives, then resolve):
    [Features › Addressing › Bookmarks](?path=/story/seq-next-features-addressing-bookmarks--bookmarks), and on p53
    [Bookmarks and links](?path=/story/seq-next-examples-p53-bookmarks-and-links--bookmarks-and-links);
  - links in a text that focus, highlight and select; one residue in two numberings; copy link:
    [Features › Addressing › Links](?path=/story/seq-next-features-addressing-links--links);
  - the selection and the focus as addresses: the status lines of the Links and Selection stories.
- **Selection**: the default gesture grammar.
  - click, shift and toggle clicks, drag-select, several ranges (Cmd/Ctrl+drag adds, Alt/Option+drag removes, Shift
    extends the anchor's range), Esc; the keyboard cursor with its arrows, Space and Enter:
    [Features › Selection](?path=/story/seq-next-features-selection--tracks);
  - projection: a selection on one section shown on another through the maps, lighter:
    [Features › Selection › Protein on its gene](?path=/story/seq-next-features-selection--protein-on-its-gene), and on
    p53 [Selection](?path=/story/seq-next-examples-p53-selection--selection);
  - the selection drawn as one band a section (column) or a box a track (tracks): the shape toggle of those stories.
- **Navigation**: pan and zoom by hand (drag, wheel, pinch), jumps that animate (focus, fit, reveal, step zooms; a cut
  under reduced motion), and the view's history of jumps: a double-click on what is focused goes back,
  Shift+double-click and `-` zoom out, `0` fits, Alt+Left, Backspace or the mouse's back button go back, Alt+Right
  forward: [Features › Selection](?path=/story/seq-next-features-selection--tracks), and on p53
  [Selection](?path=/story/seq-next-examples-p53-selection--selection).
- **Security bounds**: a spec comes from a URL, a link or an agent, so what it sizes is refused with a coded issue
  before the work starts: its size (64 MB), depth (256), query depth (64), column length and block values (2^24); the
  issues kept are bounded too (the first 10 of a code, at most 100 a node). The viewer holds them on its patches,
  previews, story steps and whole roots (a load rejects with the issues), and its store on tables and nodes: tested, not
  shown in a story.

## Planned

- **Language**:
  - a spec written with a typed builder, stored as JSON (or MessagePack for large inline data), printed as
    S-expressions;
  - validation that reports every issue;
  - versions and migrations;
- **Coordinates and maps**:
  - indices and labels; numbering schemes (Kabat, IMGT);
  - cyclic systems;
  - map kinds: identity, offset, numbering, segments, pairwise, alignment, codon, structure;
  - maps written inline, read from a source, or computed by a runtime, and held compressed;
  - direction on every run; paths and projection; codon tables;
  - structure maps (SIFTS in abstract form);
  - a section's direction (reversed, or reading along another system), collapsed introns, another system's letters on a
    section (a protein a codon wide on its gene): the Axis page.
- **Data and sources**:
  - sequences, units, copies and ligands;
  - alignments and trees, clades folded to a summary row, 10,000 rows held and millions lazy;
  - the six annotation shapes;

  - the text formats: FASTA, alignments, tables, Newick.

- **Streaming and patches**: how state changes. Patches edit the spec and are the only undoable changes; streams bring
  data and are never undone or saved.
  - patches by address, all or nothing, with inverses and a correct `diff`; undo and redo, one step a batch;
  - previews: a gesture's transient change, ending in one patch or a cancel;
  - snapshots: named patches over the base (leaving a step is navigation, not undo);
  - stories: a step as a full state or a patch (a delta from another step), a transition planned from the difference and
    sampled at any time, run on the application's clock (the animation port), scrubbed, followed by another view on the
    same plans and exported a step at a time: on p53 [Story](?path=/story/seq-next-examples-p53-story--story);
  - patches on streamed things (a bookmark on a transcript that comes and goes), and a command to keep a region on
    purpose;
  - patch streams from an agent or a host, applied in order (collaboration later);
  - every command says whether it edits the spec, changes the session or only navigates;
  - self-describing sources: format, location, index, assembly, aliases, fields;
  - one source protocol for inline and streamed data: what a source covers, and a region at a level of detail;
  - answers that carry nodes as well as tables (transcripts, nested sources, their own coordinate systems), shown by
    track templates;
  - a data store with versioned entries, pending never drawn as empty, prefetch, cancellation and byte-bounded eviction;
  - open value domains computed when the view settles; exports that wait for their data;
  - format runtimes as registry entries, bigWig as the proof, a synthetic asynchronous source for tests.
- **Layers and themes**:
  - values with points, split and log scale, pairs, matrix, grids and dot plots, logo, frames, rows;
  - fallbacks;
  - themes, residue schemes, shared domains as scale nodes, legends a host places, size channels, a reader's color-blind
    substitution: the Themes and legends page;
  - column measures for alignment schemes (Clustal X, percent identity, ESPript).
- **Views**:
  - tracks and text layouts, alignment figures;
  - blocks, rings with a window a linear detail follows, through one layout interface: the Layouts page;
  - navigators: the ruler rail on every linear section, strips, ideograms, a vertical overview, a miniature: the
    Navigators page;
  - follow and scroll groups, merged copies.
- **Interaction and editing**:
  - a configurable gesture grammar;
  - hover across linked sections, selection, the keyboard cursor;
  - focus and reveal, highlights;
  - search and queries;
  - track and group editing;
  - copying a selection's letters as shown;
  - the hover card and context menu.
- **Rendering**:
  - the resolved view and draw lists as public forms;
  - Canvas2D, SVG and PNG, the same picture;
  - a render CLI, a text-metrics port;
  - the UX foundations.
- **Ports with default adapters**: assets (Mol\*'s asset manager), text metrics, structure; archives over the assets
  port.
- **Configuration and extensions**:
  - one typed configuration (settings in layers and a generated form come later);
  - layout and spacing in presets;
  - extension descriptors;
  - node kinds and custom fields;
  - every registry open.
- **Addressing**: local, hierarchical ids; addresses as `path/(query :key value)` for nodes, locations, elements and
  rects; user-defined refs (bookmarks are refs); commands and markdown links act on addresses; copy link. Built: refs
  and bookmarks (on dynamic nodes too), addresses resolved and printed back, links, the commands, the selection and the
  focus as addresses (the Features › Addressing stories); highlights are not drawn yet.
- **Example datasets**: real public entries under permissive licences, their provenance kept as data, large variants
  generated in code: the Examples page.
- **Integration and showcases**:
  - a Nightingale reference rendering of a subset;
  - bigWig read by region, as proof that a genome format plugs in;
  - an application composing services (UniProt, PDBe, AlphaFold DB) and reading structure files;
  - the Mol\* link: a lab's position-indexed predictions mapped onto a structure's `label` and `auth` numbering.

## Deferred

Each is deferred until the trigger named for it.

- **A published JSON Schema**: until an outside reader or an agent tool needs one.
- **The Genomes milestone**: layers across sections and synteny, contact maps, a MAF over a genome, values for genomics
  (points, split, log scale), variants by sample, matrices, keyed joins, gene hierarchies, and the genome formats beyond
  bigWig: after version 1's views run.
- **Writers (FASTA, PDF)**: until the first writer extension.
- **Agent tools**: until the language description and addressing are built.
- **A React adapter**: until a React host asks.

# Region reads

Pairs, grids and matrices are read **by region**, as ranges and values are: a source answers a region at a level, the
store keeps the tiles, a layer draws what is in view. Cost follows pixels, never the data: a Hi-C of a genome, a synteny
of two genomes, thousands of links and a cohort's genotypes each read what the screen can show.

## The answer shapes

- **Pairs**: a list (each row two ends, an `id` or `row` that names it once across tiles) at the items' level, bin pairs
  (each bin pair its count, by relative strand, by `category` when the source says) at coarser ones. A request finds a
  pair from either end; a sided source (two genomes, a name listed once a side) is read by side.
- **A grid**: dense or sparse 2D tiles of the same level on both axes (`RegionRequest.y`), a symmetric grid served once
  at or above its diagonal and mirrored by the reader (Hi-C), counts summed or scores averaged going coarser.
- **A matrix**: items × states, an item a variant or a CpG at its own extent, a code (genotype) or a value (NaN missing)
  a cell; a coarse level a bin's majority class and its share, or its mean.
- **Keyed rows**: records by key, for results that are no places.

A source that serves one level (level 1) gets the others from the store's **summaries** (a pairs pyramid, grid levels,
and for a grid of several chromosomes a whole-assembly level cut from the blocks' planes), bounded in bytes.

## What a view reads

- **Pairs**: bin pairs at a couple of device pixels, always; the items once the bin pairs in view sum to at most 4,096
  (a gate with hysteresis: shown, they are kept to 1.25 times that, so a pan at the threshold never flips). A bin pair
  is one arc stroked by its count, its hover naming both bins, never a pair.
- **A grid**: the finest level whose bin is at least a device pixel, in tiles of 256 bins and a ring of tiles round the
  view. A tile not loaded draws the coarser one held under it, else a pending rect, and the plot's box never moves.
- **A matrix**: a row a state in the band, an item a rect at its extent, **a long deletion one solid bar** across the
  SNVs it covers with the short ones over it; zoomed out a bin's majority class, its share the colour's strength. The
  states are named in the label column (a row 8 px or more) as an alignment's rows are, a missing value is the missing
  colour (never the ramp's low end), and a hover reads `Sample S11 · 0/1 (heterozygous)`.
- **On a ring**: a matrix's states are thin rings, an image drawn as runs (at most a run a device column a ring), so the
  SVG is paths, never a picture.
- **Nothing in view** is no arc and no placeholder; pending is the hatched band; a layer whose wants pass the store's
  bounds says "zoom in to read this track".

## Stories

Generated in the examples (small, seeded, no file read), answered after a delay on the store's clock, the coarse levels
first, each the canvas and the SVG of one view, light and dark by the toolbar's theme:

- Pairs: [zoomed in](?path=/story/seq-next-features-region-reads-pairs--zoomed-in) (a synteny alignment between two
  assemblies in one section, items as arcs), [zoomed out](?path=/story/seq-next-features-region-reads-pairs--zoomed-out)
  (12,000 links, bin pairs by count, held and as a BEDPE one picture: the pairs level),
  [on a ring](?path=/story/seq-next-features-region-reads-pairs--on-a-ring) (the genome ring: chords between a toy
  assembly's chromosomes)
- Hi-C: [by region](?path=/story/seq-next-features-region-reads-hi-c--by-region) (a pending tile drawn coarse, then
  sharp), [over the genome](?path=/story/seq-next-features-region-reads-hi-c--over-the-genome) (an assembly's blocks on
  both axes, read by block pair)
- Matrix: [samples by positions](?path=/story/seq-next-features-region-reads-matrix--samples-by-positions) (40 samples,
  a long deletion solid), [on a ring](?path=/story/seq-next-features-region-reads-matrix--on-a-ring),
  [methylation with values missing](?path=/story/seq-next-features-region-reads-matrix--methylation-with-missing-values)
- [Zoom in to read](?path=/story/seq-next-features-region-reads-zoom-in--zoom-in-to-read): the waiting band, and links
  with nothing in view

## Not yet

- Over an assembly, a grid reads by block pair at every zoom: the whole-assembly level cut into block pairs (one want
  for the section at the fit) is not read yet.
- Held links over an assembly's blocks bin by device column past the gate, not by the pairs level (a BEDPE file is: the
  `bed` runtime serves its `pairs` by region and its bin pairs by level).
- The matrix's state header (the `order` and `hidden` of a state) is listed, not built; its names are in the label
  column.

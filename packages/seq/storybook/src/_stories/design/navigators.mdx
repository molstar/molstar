# Navigators

A navigator answers two questions at a glance: where am I in the whole, and take me there. It is one part, set by the
axes it covers, for any view whose whole does not fit: a chromosome region, a 10,000-row alignment, a long protein, a
plasmid's linear detail.

## Forms

| Form              | Covers    | Shows                                                          |
| ----------------- | --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| the ruler rail    | positions | the window as a thicker segment just below the ruler's border  |
| strip             | positions | the axis, or the section's tracks in miniature                 |
| ideogram          | positions | a chromosome's Giemsa bands, the centromere as two triangles   |
| vertical overview | both      | an alignment's rows in miniature, the rows and columns in view |
| miniature         | both      | tracks and rows, both axes whole                               |

- **A region far smaller than its chromosome** (a gene on chr17) shows on the ideogram as a mark with no grips, and a
  zoom strip under it, joined by two lines, carries the working window over the region alone.
- **The ruler rail is every linear section's navigator by default**, at no row's cost; a section without a ruler gets a
  slim rail under its tracks, and an alignment's rows a vertical rail beside them. A declared navigator, or one a host
  places, replaces it. A drag on a positions ruler pans. On an alignment's ruler only the lower 40% is the rail's, and a
  drag above it selects columns.
- **The rows rail** sits at the right edge of the rows, beside their last numbers: its window is the rows in view, a
  drag scrolls them, a grip zooms them (`]` and `[` too), a click outside centres them there. It is idle while every row
  shows. A declared vertical overview does not replace it (decided by the author, 2026-11-20): the overview sits right
  of the rail, both drawn against one window. `navigator.rowsRail` is `always` (the default), `unlessOverview` (the
  overview replaces it; one a narrow frame hides counts as hidden) or `none`. In a narrow frame the overview narrows
  from 96 to 48 px and hides below a plot of `navigator.overview.minPlotPx` (160 px), the rail staying.
- Other forms are nodes of the view in the spec, so they travel with it; the host places each part where it wants
  (`mountNavigator`), and may also add one without touching the spec. A part sits beside the view at its form's size (a
  strip across it, an overview 96 px wide, a miniature 160 × 96), never over the data or the toolbar.
- A navigator draws from what the views already summarize, and colors by the view's domains, so a value has one color in
  both.

## The window

The window is the bound section's viewport. Several leading sections on one navigator draw one window each.

- **Drag the body** to pan, **drag a grip** to zoom with the other end held; the grips are tabs just outside the ends,
  so a press in the middle always pans.
- **The cursor says which**: a resize cursor along the navigator over a grip, a hand over the body, a pointer outside.
- **Click outside** to centre the view there; **drag from outside** to sweep a region and zoom to it; double-click fits,
  or zooms back in.
- A wheel over a navigator acts as over the view, zooming about the window's centre.
- A window too small to see is drawn at least 16 px wide, centred on where it really is.
- **Idle** when the view shows everything and cannot zoom in: drawn quiet (on the rail a hairline), not a target. Where
  the view cannot be moved (a read-only story step) it is drawn the same way.
- A window moved on a follower moves its leader. A reversed section's navigator runs in display order, so a drag right
  moves the view right; collapsed introns show as notches.

## History, keys and screen readers

- A click, a sweep and a double-click are jumps: Alt+Left goes back. A drag is by hand and pushes a step only when it
  travelled farther than a window's width.
- The part is one tab stop; each window is a slider: arrows pan, Shift+arrows resize, Home and End go to the ends;
  PageDown and PageUp move between the windows, and a press on a window focuses its slider. Its value is announced in
  the section's labels ("positions 1,200–1,450 of 2,686") when the view comes to rest after the part's own move; a move
  made elsewhere announces nothing.
- A hover in a navigator shows its own readout (a position, a row, a band's name), never the view's hover.

## Hidden, and in exports

A host can turn the rails off (`navigator.form: none`), a declared navigator can be hidden by an edit, and the embedded
layout has a corner button for the reader. Hiding the ruler falls back to the slim rail, so the way to move is never
lost. An SVG export leaves navigators and rails out unless asked; when included, windows are outlines and rails plain
segments, with no handles.

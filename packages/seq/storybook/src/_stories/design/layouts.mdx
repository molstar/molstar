# Layouts

A view stacks its sections, and each section has a layout: **tracks**, **text** or **rings**, with blocks in any of
them. A ring and its linear detail are two sections of one view. The dot plot is a section of its own (see Grids and
rows).

## Strips and warps

Every layout cuts its section into straight **strips** and places each on the page with a **warp**:

| Layout | Strips                           | Warp                                |
| ------ | -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| tracks | one per block in view            | a translation                       |
| text   | one per line (a chip per ligand) | a translation                       |
| rings  | one per block, unrolled          | polar: x to an angle, y to a radius |

Layer types draw one way, over a straight axis, in every layout; the layout never knows layer kinds. Hit-testing is
placement's inverse, so every drawn mark is hit where it is drawn. A selection looks the same everywhere: a band on a
line of text, a sector across every ring.

A track without a `height` is as tall as its content needs (the lanes of its whole data), in steps of a lane, its marks
centred; tracks of one kind in a section share one height, so they read as a set and never resize on a pan. The sizing
is the section's `heights` param: `content` (this, the default), `uniform`, `fixed`, or an extension's.

## Text

- Positions in groups of ten (`group`), whole groups per line; inline labels take part in line breaking.
- Each track is a band under the letters on every line; empty bands collapse per line.
- Numbers per row (`numbers`: `start`, `end`, `both`, `none`) and group labels, on the ruler's label line, one label a
  position; frames across tracks, open where a line breaks them.
- Alignment figures in the ESPript style: each row's label on every line, its last residue's number at the end.
- Zoom is the letter size; a resize reflows with the reader's line kept at the top. A text in a host page is capped at
  `text.maxHeight` and scrolls inside, taking the wheel only while it overflows.
- A selection made in a linked view scrolls a text to it (`reveal.linked`).
- An export draws every line: a text is a figure.

## Rings

- Tracks become rings, the first outermost; the origin at `rotation` (the top by default), positions clockwise.
- Labels inside their mark when they fit, else beside: outside the ring with a leader, or in a lane above a linear
  track; one rule and one text style in both views, placed once when laid out; a label is hit as its item. A label that
  fits neither whole is cut inside with an ellipsis, keeping at least `labels.minChars` letters (4), else dropped.
- The ring's **window** is what a linear detail follows: drag it to move the detail, drag a grip to resize it; a pan of
  the detail moves the window. Either section can lead.
- A feature across the origin is one arc, selected as two runs, focused by its shortest arc.
- The window shows only when something follows the ring, and an exported map leaves it out unless asked.

## Blocks

A block is a piece of a section on a system of its own: a chain, a ligand. Blocks sit side by side (`arrange: row`) or
stacked; in text each starts a line. A one-position block (a ligand, a glycan, a drug) is a chip in every layout: a
fixed slot its label's width, which zoom does not scale, so it reads at any zoom; in text it flows like a word. Where it
binds is an optional contacts layer the application adds. Copies of one molecule merge into one block labeled ×N
(`copies: merge`), hovered for every member, or split (`each`).

## Gestures

| Where          | Zoom                                                          | Pan                                                        |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| tracks         | Cmd/Ctrl+wheel, pinch, `-`, `=` about the pointer             | wheel, the ruler rail, Space+drag                          |
| text           | the letter size, about the pointer's position; `0` the preset | wheel (while it overflows), Space+drag: lines              |
| rings          | the window narrows and widens about the pointer's angle       | wheel, Shift+Alt+arrows: the window round, past the origin |
| stacked blocks | about the block under the pointer                             | as tracks                                                  |

A drag on data selects in every layout: in reading order across lines in text, along an arc on a ring.
`selection.copyLetters` (Cmd/Ctrl+C) copies what is shown: in reading order, complemented as drawn, rows as FASTA lines.

## One viewport record

Each section has one viewport, read by its layout: a window in tracks, an anchor and a letter size in text, the window
on a ring, plus a band's own scroll and row zoom for an alignment's rows and a grid's second axis. A saved session and
the view's history keep positions, so back lands on the same place even after a layout changes.

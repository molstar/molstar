# Stories

A story is a sequence of view states over one spec, like slides. Seq\* computes each state, plans the transition between
two states from their difference, and samples it at any time. The application's clock decides what is shown and when:
Seq\* runs no animation loop of its own.

## A step is a full state

- A story's steps are kept in order, each with an id, a label, its content, its view and its timing.
- A step's content is a **patch** over the spec (small, and it follows edits of the spec) or a **full state** (a figure
  that must not move when the spec is edited). The writer picks per step, and can convert one into the other.
- **Keyframes and deltas**, as a video codec or a rewinding game stores them: a full state and a patch over the spec are
  keyframes; a patch from another step is a delta. A step is at most `story.maxChain` deltas (8) from a keyframe, so
  reaching any step costs a bounded number of applies. `story.store: smaller` picks the smallest form per step.
- States in memory share every unchanged subtree, so many states cost what differs. Sampled frames are never stored.
- Entering a step is navigation: no undo step, no change to the spec. Edits while a patch step is shown edit the spec;
  while a full-state step is shown they are refused, with a hint to edit the step.

## Transitions are planned from the difference

`plan(from, to)` returns plain data: a list of channels, each one property that changes, with its kind, its start and
end values and its time window. An application may inspect, retime, drop or add channels.

| Channel | What changes                            | Default                                                  |
| ------- | --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| view    | a section's range, the view shown       | pan when the ranges overlap, else out, across and in     |
| number  | a height, a domain, an opacity          | tweened                                                  |
| color   | a color or a theme's resolved colors    | blended in a perceptual space                            |
| enter   | a track, layer or section appears, goes | fades and grows or shrinks; the layout moves the rest    |
| order   | children reordered                      | each moves to its new place                              |
| cut     | anything else                           | switched at the channel's time (the midpoint by default) |

Data does not animate: data arriving during a transition draws in place. Reduced motion makes every channel a cut;
whether motion plays is the viewer's one setting, `view.motion` (`platform`, `cut`, `animate`), for jumps and stories alike.

## Sampling is pure; a frame is shown, never committed

`sample(plan, t)` is deterministic and allocation-free once warm: the same function for preview, scrubbing and export. A
frame is drawn as a transient layer over the shown state. It makes no patch, no revision and no undo step.

## The application owns the clock

The animation port has two faces, like the history port: the application drives Seq\* (`show(frame)` or
`progress(plan, t)`, then `clear()`), and Seq\*'s own motions (focus, reveal, a step's view) are plans handed to the
application, so a host that animates a structure's camera runs both on one clock. The default composition runs requested
plans on the page's animation frames; the headless one runs a fake clock frame by frame, which is how a story is
exported.

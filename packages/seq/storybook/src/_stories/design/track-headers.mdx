# Track headers

A track's header is its line in the label column: its name, its states and its actions. A section's header is a line
above its first track, drawn only when a section entry gives it a slot. Hidden tracks are the application's: the view
draws nothing for them.

## Actions are entries

Every action (hide, color, layers, move) is a registry entry that names a command, the built-ins included. A host adds
its own (such as "color by this track" for a structure viewer) or leaves a built-in out. Actions show in fixed slots
while the pointer or the keyboard cursor is on the track, with the "⋯" at the column's end and the slots left of it; on
touch, a tap on the label shows them and a tap on a slot runs it. Every entry is also in the track's menu.

## States are chips

What is not shown is a count chip: "12 filtered", "1 layer hidden". On a short track or in a narrow column they become
small marks, all of them or none, their words in the mark's card. A click undoes one in one step (the "n filtered" chip
even in a viewer without the header actions); its menu brings back one thing at a time. A hidden track leaves no chip
and no line (hiding never moves the view): the application lists the hidden tracks and restores them (the toolbar's eye
button, `mountHiddenTracks`), and the menu's "Show hidden" does too.

## Drawn or the host's own

By default the slots and chips are drawn on the canvas, so they scroll and export with the picture. A host may instead
place its own DOM parts over the same header geometry, which the viewer publishes; Seq\* ships a reference set built on
`@molstar/ui`. Exported figures look the same either way.

## The menu

The "⋯" slot, the menu key or a secondary click open the menu for what is under the pointer: an item's entries first,
then its track's and its section's. Where a secondary click opens it is the composing application's choice; with Shift
it is always the browser's own menu. A submenu (Color, Layers, Show hidden) opens beside its row, never in the menu's
place, when clicked or when the pointer rests on its row; the menu's clicks and hover never reach the view below it. The
Layers submenu ends with a separator and Show all and Hide all (one edit each, disabled with their reason when they
would change nothing); the legend's eye menu is the same menu.

# Structure syncing

A sequence view beside a structure viewer: hover, selection and focus cross both ways, and a track's colors can be
painted onto the structure. Syncing is not part of the viewer: the viewer publishes its events and takes commands as it
does for any application, and Seq\* adds a small library on top. Neither viewer imports the other.

## Translation

Pure functions carry Seq\*'s locations onto the **units** of a complex (a chain instance, a ligand) and back, through
the maps. They give the application the facts it needs to speak its viewer's language (MolViewSpec selectors, Mol\*'s
loci): chain, operator, observed residues and author numbering. Unobserved residues are never sent, and a selection too
large to send residue by residue becomes whole units. They run without a viewer, in Node too.

## Wiring

One helper, `linkStructure(viewer, side)`, connects a viewer to the application's few callbacks (highlight, select,
focus, and the structure viewer's own events) and returns a function that disconnects them. It keeps the two sides from
echoing each other, sends a dragged selection at most once a frame, and clears both when either goes away.

- **Hover** lights the same residues in both views. Hovering one copy in 3D lights that chain and its reference
  sequence, never the sibling copies.
- **Selection** made in 3D replaces the sequence selection and pans to it, keeping the zoom.
- **Focus**: a double-click moves the camera, and back and forward bring it along, on one clock.
- **Color by this track** is an action the application adds to a track's header: Seq\* returns the track's colors as
  runs on the units, and the application builds its viewer's state from them.

## Two maps of one chain

A reference sequence can reach a chain through SIFTS and through an alignment at once. Each map may carry a
**priority**: the higher-ranked one is taken everywhere. A section may name the map it goes **via**, so a figure can
compare both side by side. A path is ranked by its maps' priorities, weakest first, so a map both paths share never
hides the rivals. Equal priorities stay ambiguous and are reported, never chosen silently; a `via` that names a map off
the path is a warning.

## In the Storybook

The structure viewer here is a small fake: chains as rows of residue cells, with its own MolViewSpec translation and an
optional latency. A real 3D viewer will use the same library and the same tests.

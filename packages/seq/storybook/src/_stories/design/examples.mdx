# Examples

A few datasets, each rich enough for many views. A view is one spec over its dataset: one way of looking, or one editing
experience. Views appear in this group as they are built.

## Datasets

| Dataset         | Holds                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| p53             | the TP53 protein with features and variants, its gene on the minus strand with exons, the gene's region read by region with a conservation signal, structure chains with unobserved residues, orthologs                                           |
| Globins         | a family alignment with its tree, hemoglobin (α2β2 with heme), and a generated 10,000-row alignment                                                                                                                                               |
| Antibody        | a Fab numbered Kabat and IMGT, its CDRs, and a designed variant with indels against the wild type                                                                                                                                                 |
| Plasmid         | a cyclic construct with features and a coding sequence across the origin, on both strands, and restriction sites                                                                                                                                  |
| tRNA            | base pairs, nested and crossing, modified bases, and a covariance alignment                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Complex         | CDK2 with cyclin A: a crystal structure with ATP (two copies of the complex), a predicted model with its confidence and PAE, the interface and its contacts, and the ligands AlphaFill transplants into CDK2's AlphaFold model, with their scores |
| Genomes (later) | two small assemblies with an inversion and a translocation, genes, a signal track and a variant matrix, read by region                                                                                                                            |

## Views

Each dataset carries many views, for example:

- **p53**:
  - a text figure with feature underlines and variant marks;
  - tracks with features, values and secondary structure;
  - the protein on its gene through codons, introns collapsed, on the minus strand;
  - structure chains linked, unobserved residues gray, copies merged and split;
  - the gene's region, region by region, with its conservation signal;
  - the minus-strand gene's features in both directions;
  - orthologs with hover across rows;
  - search, results stepping and bookmarks;
  - a story in snapshots.
- **Globins**:
  - the family with a conservation logo, rows ordered by the tree;
  - collapsed clades with a summary;
  - an ESPript-style figure;
  - the hemoglobin complex with heme chips and copy groups;
  - 10,000 rows, loaded lazily, with a keyboard cursor.
- **Antibody**:
  - Kabat and IMGT numbering side by side;
  - the design against the wild type, with indel carets;
  - editing CDR bookmarks, with undo.
- **Plasmid**:
  - a ring with a linear detail that follows its window;
  - a feature across the origin, selected and focused by the shortest arc;
  - the rotated linear view.
- **tRNA**:
  - arcs, nested and crossing;
  - a base-pair dot plot with crosshair hover and rectangle selection;
  - the covariance alignment with its pairs.
- **Complex**:
  - the chains with the interface residues, and the contacts as arcs across the chains;
  - the PAE as a dot plot over the same window as the tracks: hover lights both residues, a dragged rectangle selects
    two ranges;
  - confidence and secondary structure tracks, unobserved residues gray;
  - the ligands after the polymers: ATP as one position or per heavy atom, AlphaFill's transplants with their scores,
    and the residues a chosen ligand contacts;
  - a text figure with ligand chips that flow like words;
  - the two copies merged into one block labeled ×2, and split again;
  - the interface bookmarked, with undo.
- **Genomes** (a later milestone):
  - synteny ribbons with an inversion;
  - a dot plot of one genome against the other;
  - signals by region, many from one file;
  - a Manhattan plot colored by LD;
  - a variant matrix by sample, and methylation by region.

Editing is a view too: add a bookmark, hide or reorder a track, change a theme, record a snapshot. Each comes with undo
and redo, a preview that ends in one change or a cancel, and the printed spec updating beside it.

## Showcases

Three pages compose what an application would, rather than a core feature:

- **Services**: UniProt, PDBe and AlphaFold DB as sources, from recorded responses, with a switch to go live.
- **Predictions onto a structure**: a lab's per-position values on a plain sequence, mapped onto a structure through its
  numbering (an offset, insertion codes, unobserved loops, an expression tag), colored on the sequence and in Mol\*,
  with hover and selection both ways.
- **Addressing**: a text whose links focus, highlight and select in the view and in the structure; copy link; one
  location in several numberings; rectangles from a dot plot and an alignment; bookmarks as refs.

## Every view, three ways

- **Here**: drawn on a canvas and as SVG, side by side.
- **In a test**: it validates, resolves, lays out and renders to SVG, and its printed spec is recorded.
- **As files**: the JSON spec and its data files, which a reader without Seq\* code can open.

The data is small and committed with the Storybook. Nothing here depends on the network.

# Across sections

A **bridge** joins two neighbouring sections of a view: a band between them whose `pairs` layers draw each pair as a
**ribbon** from its extent on the upper section to its extent on the lower. Two genomes stacked with their alignment
between them is synteny; three stacked with two bridges is a chain. A bridge has no axis of its own: it is drawn from
its sides, and moving it moves them.

## What a bridge is

- **`bridge`** under a view: `a` and `b` its two sections (neighbours in the stack, each a row of tracks: a ring, a text
  or a grid is no side), `label` (else "a to b"), `height` (96 px), `lock` (`none` or `px`), `hidden` (kept in the spec,
  its room collapsed). Its children are `pairs` layers: their data's `a` end on the bridge's `a` side, `b` on `b`, by
  the systems each side shows (both ways with `mirror`, one genome against itself).
- **A ribbon** is a pair: its two ends at their true x on each side (a reversed block gives its first position the
  larger x), its sides cubics with vertical tangents, crossed when its corresponding positions run opposite ways (the
  strand by geometry, so reversing a block untwists it), at least a device pixel wide, filled at 0.35 in the colour of
  its upper end's chromosome. An end whose position no block shows is a **stub**: a quarter-height taper.
- **What is read**: each side its own window at its own level; past 4,096 ribbons in view, bin pairs (their opacity by
  the bases aligned), each drawn once; a side whose tiles are pending hatches its edge.
- **Lazy**: the bridge's layout, its ribbons and the viewer's bridge parts load the first time a spec holds a bridge; a
  view without one pays nothing.

## Moving two sections joined by a bridge

- **Each side moves alone** by default, as JBrowse's linear synteny view does.
- **`lock: px`**: a pan of either side by some px pans the other by the same px; a zoom zooms the other by the same
  factor about the same page x. Each section is moved once a gesture, by the first relation reaching it (follow, scroll
  group, lock), so a lock a follow already carries moves nothing more.
- **A drag or a wheel over the band** moves both sides, whatever the lock; it selects nothing.
- **`bridge.align`** (Shift+A at a side): the other side jumps to the hull of what the ribbons in view reach, the far
  chromosome holding the most aligned bases; a middle genome brings both neighbours in one step; back undoes it.

## Hover, selection and the keyboard

- **Hover a ribbon**: its card names both ends ("peach Pp02:1,200,001–1,450,000 ↔ grape chr2:…"), its strand and its
  aligned length.
- **`bridge.selectAcross`**: the far ends of the ribbons with an end in the selection join it, one change.
- **The keyboard cursor** crosses a band with ↓ and ↑, landing on the image of its position through the topmost ribbon
  covering it.
- **The lock toggle** (`bridge.lock`) is an edit: one undo step.

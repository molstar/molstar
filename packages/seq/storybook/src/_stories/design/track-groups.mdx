# Track groups

A writer puts a section's tracks under named groups ("Features", "Variants", "Signals"); a reader folds a group to one
row that still shows what its tracks hold, and opens it again, as often as they scroll. A section of 30 tracks then
reads as six rows.

## A group is the writer's, its fold the reader's

A `group` is a node of the spec (language `0.11.0`): a section's child holding tracks, one level deep, with a `label`,
the opening state `collapsed`, `hidden`, and `summary` (`auto`, `none`, or one of its own tracks). Its tracks are its
children, so a track in a group has the address `/views/v/<section>/<group>/<track>`.

Folding is session state, as a tree's clades are: a click on the header folds or opens one group and is not an undo
step; Collapse all and Expand all are jumps of the view's history, so Alt+Left brings the folds back with the window.
"Keep these folds as the opening state" writes the folds shown into the groups' `collapsed`, one patch.

## The header and the folded row

An open group draws a header line above its first track: a chevron, its label, and on hover its slots and "⋯"; its
tracks' labels are indented. A folded group is one row: its label column the header with a quiet count ("3 tracks"),
its band the summary.

The summary draws each of the group's tracks in its layer's summary form, in one band: ranges, secondary structure and
variants in one shared lane without labels; pairs as the ticks of their ends; values as thin strips, at most four; an
alignment as its consensus row. Letters, logos and rulers are left out. A writer may name one of the group's tracks as
its summary instead: that track shows only while the group is folded.

## Elsewhere

Text and ring layouts draw every group open, with no header line. An exported figure draws the folds it is given. A
ranges track can also be drawn in one lane with its labels off (`lanes: 'one'`), the form a folded group draws its
ranges in.

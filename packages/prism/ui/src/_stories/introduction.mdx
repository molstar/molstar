# Mol\* prism-ui

Prism, the next Mol\* UI: the viewer (`@molstar/prism-viewer`) on a real Mol\* plugin, and the parts it is made of
(`@molstar/prism-ui`).

## Where to look

- **Overview**: this page; **Foundations**: the tokens (surfaces, text, states, brand colors), how surfaces layer, the
  themes, type and spacing.
- **Viewer**: the real viewer (`createPrismViewer`) over the repository's example files or the network (said on each
  story):
  - **Shell**: the whole app: loaded, the command menu, the canvas background, the pinned panel, the compact layout,
    expand and fullscreen, Help, a card opened at a section.
  - **Home**, **Structure panel**, **Volumes**, **Playback**, **Assistant**: a panel or a card, at what it shows (the
    tree and the inspector of what is picked, a map's levels and transfer function, the playback card and the Story).
  - **Data**: one story per kind of data (structures, models, assemblies, maps, particles, shapes, MolViewSpec).
  - **MVS stories**: the MolViewSpec stories example, played in the viewer.
  - **With extensions**: the viewer as the app composes it, with Mol\*'s extensions.
- **Components**: the parts over stand-in models, for their states one by one: buttons, icons, selects, the **Params
  form** (every PD control in its states, on a panel and in a well), the playback card's rows.
- **Internals**: the DOM runtime (bindings, keyed lists, conditionals) and performance checks, for working on prism-ui
  itself.

Switch the theme (light, dark, blue, system) in the toolbar; the chrome over the canvas follows the canvas background on
its own.

## Principles

- **Canvas first, quiet chrome.** Flat surfaces separated by tone and spacing, few lines; glass over the canvas.
- **One accent, for actions and the picked item.** Settings, switches and sliders are neutral.
- **Icons where they say something words do not**: icon-only controls, state, top-level tree rows, the command menu; not
  next to labels.
- **One text size in panels** (12 px), every control in one column, at the same text inset.
- **The command menu teaches the rest**: every action is a command, with the names people would type.
- **Mol\*'s ideas, restyled**: its interaction patterns stay (action footers, presets, the state tree), the look is new.

## The design

The designs live in `.v6/prism-ui`: `design/shell.md` (layout, panels, what each tab holds), `design/commands.md`,
`design/styling.md` (tokens and rules), `design/architecture.md`, and `plan.md` (the phases, then the review gate and
the build against a real plugin, with `apps/prism` to show it early).

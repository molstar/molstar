# Mol\* prism-ui

The next Mol\* plugin UI (`@molstar/prism-ui`), as mocks: the shell, its panels and the canvas chrome, built on their
view models with mocked data. They settle the UX before the build against a real plugin; nothing here talks to Mol\*
yet.

## Where to look

- **Overview**: this page; **Foundations**: the tokens (surfaces, text, states, brand colors), how surfaces layer, the
  themes, type and spacing.
- **Shell**: the whole app. **Loaded** is the usual state; **Empty** the start (the wordmark on the canvas); others show
  a session with playback, many structures, an EMDB map, a dark canvas, the compact layout, the command menu, the pinned
  panel, remote states.
- **Views**: single tabs at panel width (Home, Structure).
- **Canvas**: what floats over the canvas (playback).
- **Components**: the parts: buttons, icons, and the **Params form**: every PD control in its states, on a panel and in
  a well.
- **Internals**: the DOM runtime (bindings, keyed lists, conditionals), for working on prism-ui itself.

Switch the theme (light, dark, blue, system) in the toolbar; the chrome over the canvas follows the canvas background on
its own.

## Principles

- **Canvas first, quiet chrome.** Flat surfaces separated by tone and spacing, few lines; glass over the canvas.
- **One accent, for actions and the picked item.** Settings, switches and sliders are neutral.
- **Icons where they say something words do not**: icon-only controls, state, top-level tree rows, the command menu; not
  next to labels.
- **One text size in panels** (12 px), every control in one column, at the same text inset.
- **The command menu teaches the rest**: every action is a command, with the names people would type.
- **Mol\*'s ideas, restyled**: its interaction patterns stay (action footers, presets, the state tree), the look is new.

## The design

The designs live in `.v6/prism-ui`: `design/shell.md` (layout, panels, what each tab holds), `design/commands.md`,
`design/styling.md` (tokens and rules), `design/architecture.md`, and `plan.md` (the phases, then the review gate and
the build against a real plugin, with `apps/prism` to show it early).

# Lane CS: p53's chromosome

**Goal** (the author, 2026-11-19: "we should also add a "genome-level" example(s)", "let's do chromosome scale this
slice"): build the view [examples.md](../../../design/examples.md), "p53's chromosome", designs: chromosome 17 at its
GRCh38 length, generated by region and never stored (letters by `PCG` seeded per block, about 1,200 genes with exons as
dynamic nodes, a smooth signal by bin-width levels), TP53 at 7,668,402–7,687,550 on the minus strand carrying the
dataset's existing gene (`tp53-gene.seq`). The view: a ruler in Mb, genes, the signal, the letters when they fit, an
overview navigator showing the window; opened fit to the whole chromosome; zoom to TP53 (double-click on the gene) and
down to its letters. Its story under Examples › p53, its headless twin, its index entry; the Design page already lists
it.

Designs to read: examples.md (p53's chromosome, Configuration: `chromosome.length`, `.genes`, `.seed`, options of the
generator); [sources.md](../../../design/sources.md) and [data-store.md](../../../design/data-store.md) (the source
protocol, levels, prefetch, eviction); [dynamic-nodes.md](../../../design/dynamic-nodes.md);
[performance.md](../../../design/performance.md) (Levels and a frame's cost; Scale); [navigators.md](../../../design/navigators.md).
ux-foundations' matrix cells: zoom and pan on a leading section, focus (double-click), the navigator's window, 0 fits,
hover (the card). The existing by-region view, `packages/seq/examples/src/p53/gene-by-region.ts` and `region.ts`, is
the pattern: reuse its format runtime and helpers, generalize them rather than copy.

**Minimal code**: a generator by region and a view spec; the format runtime shared with `region.ts` where it can be.
Nothing new in `@molstar/seqx-core` unless the view cannot be built without it: then stop and report what and why (a
missing piece at 83 Mb is a finding, not something to patch around). The ruler's Mb, kb and bp labels from
`@molstar/core/util/number` as they are.

**Two consumers**: the canvas and the SVG export of the same view, live in one test. **Drawing against the data**: at the
fit view, at TP53 and at its letters, every gene in view has its marks and every drawn gene is hit; across a tile edge.

**Performance representative**: one quick case (the fit view and TP53's letters resolve and lay out, regions asked only
for the view and its margin); a `*.quick.ts` perf case (a warm pan at the fit view and at a density between two levels
allocates nothing past the layout's bound); a heavy `itWithin` bound that a frame at 83 Mb is within 1.2 times the
p53 gene view's (performance.md's flat frame time). The quick lane's folders stay under 2 s (QT3 just brought them down).

**Tests** (fail without the view): the generator is deterministic by region (the same region from two orders of
requests is equal); TP53's span equals `tp53-gene.seq`; the store requests only the view's regions and evicts past its
bound, never the view; the navigator's window; double-click on TP53 focuses it; the hover card names a gene.

**Not in scope**: open by name, gene hierarchies, a second assembly, bigWig (the Genomes dataset and the fourth slice).
RK runs beside you in `packages/seq/core` and `packages/seq/viewer` (the keyboard cursor's rows): stay in
`packages/seq/examples` and `packages/seq/storybook`, and touch core or viewer only to report.

**Model**: strong (opus). **Base**: `packages-seq` at the commit this brief is in: `git checkout -B seq/cs-chromosome
<that commit>`. Storybook port 6020 (your own; stop it after).

**Checks**: as [hc-hover-card.md](hc-hover-card.md)'s; `pnpm test:heavy packages/seq/examples` for your bound only,
after `pgrep -fl "vitest.*heavy"`. Look at the view at the fit, at TP53 and at its letters, in light and dark.

**Headers**, **hot files** and **stop when** (any public API or a `@molstar/seqx-core` change): as every lane's (see
[qt-quick-lane.md](qt-quick-lane.md)).

**Report**: commits; checks; folder durations; sizes; the perf numbers; what you saw; drift; followups; a
`.v6/changelogs/` entry's text.

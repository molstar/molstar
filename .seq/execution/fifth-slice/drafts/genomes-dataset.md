# The Genomes dataset: its views, what each needs, the generator and the fetches

Lane GD, stage 0, read only. Draft, 2026-11-21, from [examples.md](../../../design/examples.md) (Genomes, 112, 124, 151,
245, 413 to 414, 446), the plan's Gate 0 (decided as recommended) and the five drafts: GA
([genome-coordinates](genome-coordinates.md)), RR ([region-reads](region-reads.md)), GF
([genome-formats](genome-formats.md)), LX ([across-sections](across-sections.md)), RN
([ring-extension](ring-extension.md)). A design owner is named per need; **GAP** marks a need no draft serves.

## Intent

One dataset, `genomes` in `packages/seq/examples`, that every view of the milestone reads: committed fixtures a few
hundred kB each (the examples' `fixtures.maxFileBytes` 1 MB and `maxDatasetBytes` 2 MB, kept), generated by a seeded
module and written by the record script; one real slice a format for the conformance suites (Gate 0, 7); and a
performance fixture that is never stored (a whole human genome by region, heavy). The views are builder modules as in
the other datasets; each view's test drives the cells of the interaction matrix it names.

## The generator: outline

`packages/seq/examples/src/genomes/` (modules under 800 lines, §module-size), one `generateGenomes(options)` returning
bytes and truths, one record script case `genomes` (`scripts/record.ts`, run by hand) writing the fixtures and their
`sources.json` rows with licence `LicenseRef-seqx-generated`, and the real slices as their own rows (CC0, NCBI public
data, or what the author clears). Randomness only from core's `PCG` and `hash3` (as `p53/chromosome-data.ts`), a
stream per concern seeded by `(seed, concern, index)`, so adding a concern never moves another's bytes; no clock, no
`Math.random`. Output byte-identical on every run, checked by a test comparing checksums to the committed fixtures
(the way the other datasets' fixture tests do).

Steps (each a module, each exporting its truth for the tests):
1. **Assemblies**: toy A: `chr1` 300,000, `chr2` 200,000, `chr3` 120,000, `chrM` 16,569 (cyclic), `HLA-A*01:01:01:01`
   3,503, and a 20,000 gap of N inside chr1; toy B: the same names unprefixed (`1`, `2`, `3`, `MT`) so the alias rule is
   exercised, an 80,000 inversion on chr1, a 60,000 translocation from chr3 to chr2. Letters: GC share by region, a
   few repeats; the aligned runs share a base sequence, with 1 % substitutions and small indels.
2. **Alignment truth**: runs (a, b, strand, CIGAR with `S`, `H`, `N`, one CIGAR covering part of a sequence), written
   as PAF, as a chain file, and as a pairs table; the liftover's truth positions.
3. **Genes**: 150 on A, 3 levels (gene, transcript, exon), both strands, names (`TOY0001`...), a name index; GFF3 and GTF
   forms of one model.
4. **Signal**: a bedGraph with 6 sample columns and a TPM-like column, smooth waves plus noise, a gap of missing values.
5. **GWAS**: about 6,000 SNPs, `p` from a mixture (most uniform, 20 peaks), an LD table for one lead (80 neighbours).
6. **Variants**: 40 samples by 2,000 sites, the deletion, the haploid, the no-call; bgzipped and tabix-indexed.
7. **Methylation**: 20 samples by 250 CpG, 8 % missing, wide and long.
8. **Results**: 400 transcripts keyed by id, dIF and padj, a fixed 30 under 0.05.
9. **Links** (the ring): 12,000 BEDPE from the typed GRCh38 lengths, in memory in tests, committed only if under the
   file bound (about 700 kB: held as a fixture, or generated at load: decide in GX).
10. **Hi-C and dot plot grids**: bin the alignment and the links into two levels in the `seq` format.

Sizes (committed, estimated; the generator's test asserts each under bound): FASTA bgzipped with `.fai` and `.gzi`
about 170 kB; 2bit about 160 kB; PAF 12 kB; chain 10 kB; GFF3 plus GTF 90 kB; bedGraph (bgzipped, tabix) 120 kB;
GWAS and LD 90 kB; VCF bgzipped with `.tbi` 60 kB; methylation 70 kB; results 25 kB; BEDPE 12,000 links 700 kB or none;
cytobands 4 kB (real). Total about 800 kB without the links, 1.5 MB with: under the 2 MB dataset bound. The real slices
below are recorded separately as pages in the formats' own `_test` fixtures, each its own row; their sizes are asked
with the fetch.

Heavy performance fixture, never stored: a whole human genome by region (the plan's M5, performance.md): GRCh38's 24
lengths summing to 3.1 Gb as an adapter in the style of `p53/chromosome-data.ts` generalised from one chromosome: letters a 64 KiB tile
at a time from `hash3(seed, chrom, tile)`, about 20,000 genes from the same stream, a GC signal with exact bin means,
a 1,000,000-point GWAS and a 12,000-link set by region. Tests per chromosome (heavy, `itWithin`), a whole-genome fit
under 4,096 blocks (GA), and a quick representative at 3 Mb.
